## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Descend into darkness. Collect legendary loot. Face the dragon. Die. Try again.

**Dungeon Bolt** is a challenging roguelite dungeon crawler where every run is different. Navigate through 5 floors of monster-infested dungeons, collect powerful weapons and armor, manage your resources wisely, and face the ultimate challenge: a dragon boss waiting at the bottom.

🗡️ **CLASSIC ROGUELITE GAMEPLAY**
Permadeath means every decision matters. Fight or flee. Spend gold on upgrades or save for better equipment. Take risks for rare loot or play it safe. One wrong move and you start from floor 1 — but you'll be wiser next time.

💎 **11 UNIQUE ENEMY TYPES**
From harmless slimes to deadly demons, each enemy has its own behavior and attack pattern. Learn their quirks, exploit their weaknesses, and adapt your strategy on the fly:
• Slimes — Easy to kill, but they multiply
• Skeletons — Slow but heavily armored
• Bats — Fast and unpredictable
• Zombies — Tough and relentless
• Ghosts — Phase through walls
• Spiders — Poison damage over time
• Goblins — Steal your gold
• Orcs — High damage, low speed
• Dark Knights — Elite warriors with shields
• Demons — Teleport and summon minions
• Dragon — The final boss. Good luck.

⚔️ **LOOT SYSTEM**
Find weapons, armor, potions, and special items scattered throughout the dungeon:
• **Weapons:** Swords, axes, bows, daggers (each with unique stats)
• **Armor:** Leather, chainmail, plate (affects defense & movement speed)
• **Potions:** Health, stamina, invisibility, berserker rage
• **Special Items:** Keys, scrolls, lucky charms

🏪 **SHOP ROOMS**
Every few floors, stumble upon a mysterious shopkeeper selling rare items. Spend your hard-earned gold wisely — you can't take it with you when you die!

🏆 **ACHIEVEMENTS**
Complete in-game challenges to unlock achievements:
• Speed runner (complete all 5 floors in under 10 minutes)
• Pacifist (reach floor 3 without killing any enemies)
• Hoarder (collect 1000 gold in a single run)
• Dragon slayer (defeat the dragon boss)
• Ironman (complete the game without using potions)
