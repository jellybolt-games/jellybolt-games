## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Conquer the board. Outsmart the AI. Dominate the grid. ⚔️

**Code Conquest** is a fast-paced turn-based strategy game where you compete for territory control on an 8×8 grid. Every move matters. Every tile counts. One wrong decision and the AI will crush you.

🎯 **SIMPLE TO LEARN, HARD TO MASTER**
The rules are simple:
• You and the AI take turns placing markers on the grid
• Capture adjacent tiles to expand your territory
• Surround enemy tiles to convert them
• Control 50%+ of the board to win

But don't be fooled — the AI is ruthless. It learns your patterns, blocks your strategies, and punishes mistakes. Can you beat it?

🧠 **3 AI DIFFICULTY LEVELS**

**Easy Mode**
The AI makes random moves. Perfect for learning the game mechanics. You should win 80%+ of the time if you're playing smart.

**Medium Mode**
The AI uses basic strategy: prioritizes center control, blocks obvious threats, and capitalizes on your mistakes. Most players get stuck here.

**Hard Mode**
The AI is a tactical genius. It predicts your moves 3-4 turns ahead, sets traps, and exploits every weakness. Only 5% of players beat Hard Mode on their first try. Are you one of them?

⚔️ **STRATEGIC DEPTH**

**Opening Game**
Control the center. The player who controls the middle 16 tiles usually wins. But rushing the center leaves your flanks vulnerable...

**Mid Game**
Expand or defend? Aggro or defensive play? Every tile you capture must be defended. Overextend and the AI will cut you off.

**End Game**
One wrong move can flip the entire board. Clutch plays, desperate gambits, and nail-biting finishes. This is where champions are made.
