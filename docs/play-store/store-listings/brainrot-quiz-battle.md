How chronically online are you? 🧠💀 Find out in the ULTIMATE brainrot quiz!

💻 **Tech** — Do you actually know how the internet works, or do you just use it? Questions about AI, programming, cybersecurity, and tech memes.

🌐 **Internet Culture** — Memes, trends, lore. Only true brainrot survivors will ace this. Test your knowledge of viral moments, famous copypastas, and internet history.

🔬 **Science** — Plot twist: science is lowkey bussin. No cap. Questions about biology, physics, chemistry, and space that'll blow your mind.

🎬 **Movies & TV** — From Marvel to memes, test your pop culture knowledge.

🎵 **Music** — Viral songs, artist trivia, and music memes. Are you a true music head?

⏱️ **15-SECOND TIMER**
No googling. No lifelines. No asking ChatGPT. Just pure brainpower (or brainrot). Answer before the clock runs out or take the L.

📊 **SCORE TRACKING & STREAKS**
Track your score, build win streaks, and flex on your friends. Every correct answer builds your streak multiplier for MASSIVE points. One wrong answer? Streak resets. No mercy.

🏅 **SHARE YOUR SCORE**
Screenshot your results and post them everywhere. Let the world know your brainrot level. Are you a NPC or a main character?
